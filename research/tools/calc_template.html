Tetration Explorer

sexpb(x) for continuous bases — via the base atlas (e-anchor + Φ modes), no server, pure table math.
Move the mouse over the plot to read values.
Ranks 5 and up: curves precomputed with the fork on a grid of bases. Odd ranks are the regular iteration at the left real fixed point of the rank below (pentation at the fixed point of sexp_b on (−2,−1)); even ranks are regular below their critical base (η₅ = 1.73736 for the hexation, η₇ = 1.83976 for the octation) and Kneser above. ~6 digits at table bases, less between them; no curve between two bases on different sides of a critical base.
Engine: gp-tetration base atlas (Kneser tetration, fatou.gp by Sheldonison; fork + atlas 2026). Accuracy of this demo: ~7–9 digits at table bases (incl. b = 2, e, 10), ~4–5 digits between them, ~2–3 near the edge b→e^{1/e}; the underlying library delivers up to 972 proven digits.
